Help

Get started in four quick steps.

1

Pick what to study

Choose a Subject, then optionally a Unit or Topics. Leave topics unselected to study everything.

2

Narrow it down

Tap ⋯ More topics, tick a topic or expand it to choose individual subtopics.

3

Study the cards

Tap the card to flip it. Use the arrows or swipe to move on, then mark Know it or Review.

4

Test yourself

Open More options to turn on quick quizzes, pick the question type or shuffle your cards.

How do I choose what to study?

Select a subject at the top, then narrow it down with units and topics. Tap "More topics" to pick specific subtopics. If nothing is selected, you'll see every card in that subject.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your Known and Review cards and your quiz score are saved in your browser on this device, so they'll still be here next time you visit. They won't carry over to a different device or browser.

How do I reset my progress?

Clear this website's data in your browser settings. This removes your saved Known and Review cards and your quiz score.

What do "Know it" and "Review" do?

They let you mark how you're going. "Know it" means you're confident with the card, and "Review" flags it for more practice. Your totals appear at the top of the page.

How do the quick quizzes work?

Open "More options" to have a quiz pop up every few cards. You can choose multiple choice, short answer or a mix. For multiple choice, press A, B, C or D on your keyboard, then Enter to continue.

Why can't I find a card I'm looking for?

Check that the right subject, unit and topic are selected and that the search box is empty. Press "Clear" to reset your filters.

Can I use this on my phone?

Yes. Swipe left or right on the card to move between cards, and use Full screen for a distraction-free view.

Is my data shared with anyone?

No. Your progress is stored only on your own device and isn't sent anywhere.
