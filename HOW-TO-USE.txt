 b) Replace everything inside <div class="legend-items"> ... </div> with nothing,
    give it id="legendItems", then add this just above initialiseFilters(); at the bottom:
        document.getElementById("legendItems").innerHTML = SUBJECTS.map(s =>
            `<div class="legend-item"><span class="legend-dot" style="background:${s.colour}"></span>${s.name}</div>`
